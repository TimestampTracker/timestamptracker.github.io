Please include the following information when reporting an issue:

  • A clear description of the issue.
  • The command you used.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue.
  • Expected behaviour.
  • Actual behaviour.
  • Bot version, available through /about.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Why are there no matching events?

Check that messages contain native Discord timestamps, the events fall within your selected time window, and both you and the bot have permission to view the channel and read its message history.

How many messages can Timestamp Tracker search?

Up to 500 recent messages in the current server text channel.

Can it search multiple channels?

Not currently. Searches are limited to the channel where the command is used.

Can other members see my results?

Search results are private to the requesting user.

Does it support my time zone?

Displayed Discord timestamps adjust to your local time. Calendar searches default to UTC unless another supported time zone is selected.

Why have the navigation buttons stopped working?

Search navigation expires after three minutes of inactivity. Run the search command again to refresh your results.

Why is the bot offline?

Timestamp Tracker currently operates on a phone during private testing. Temporary interruptions may occur, and uptime is not guaranteed.

Can I install the bot?

Public installation is not yet available. Register your interest through our early access programme.
