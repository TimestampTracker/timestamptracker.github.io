Common questions

Why are there no matches?

Make sure messages use native Discord timestamps, the event is within your selected time window and both you and the bot can read the channel history.

Why is the bot offline?

The bot currently runs on a phone during testing. It may be temporarily unavailable. No uptime guarantee applies.
