Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · 27 November 2026 · Applies to Timestamp Tracker 1.0.2 and this website.

Timestamp Tracker is an independent, Australia-based Discord utility operated by its developer. Contact: timestamptracker@gmail.com.

1. Information processed

When you use the bot, Discord supplies command inputs, user and channel identifiers, server context and permission information. To perform a requested search, the bot retrieves up to 500 recent messages from the current authorised server text channel. These may include message text, embedded titles and fields, timestamps, message identifiers and links, author type, and attachment or image metadata. Discord may supply information about other channel members in those messages. The bot uses this information only as needed to provide the requested search and related functionality.

When a server manager creates a test event, the bot processes the supplied title, time and optional image and posts a labelled event in the channel. The image is transferred to Discord for that post.

2. Purposes

Information is processed to check permissions, match and sort timestamps, apply filters, generate previews, navigate results, limit repeated requests and troubleshoot errors. We do not sell Discord message data, use it for advertising, build user profiles or train AI models on it. We do not ask for your personal Discord token or password.

3. Storage and retention

The current bot does not maintain a permanent message-history database. Search results and identifiers needed for pagination are held temporarily in runtime memory. Preview data is cleared when dismissed or when the interactive view times out after three minutes of inactivity. Cooldown and connection information may also exist in memory; operational logs may contain connection and error information. Restarting the bot clears its runtime memory. Discord may retain the original messages, bot responses and test posts under its own policies; a paging timeout does not delete these from Discord.

If you email support, we receive your email address and information you choose to send. We retain correspondence only as reasonably necessary to address the request, resolve disputes or meet applicable obligations. Please do not email tokens or unnecessary sensitive information.

4. Sharing and services

Discord receives command responses and may render or fetch original image URLs; external image hosts may receive requests as part of that process. Search responses are designed to be visible only to the requesting user. Test events are intentionally posted publicly within the selected channel, subject to channel permissions. GitHub Pages hosts this website, and Google provides the support mailbox. These providers may process technical or contact data under their own policies, including outside Australia. The bot currently runs on the operator’s phone; a change to external bot hosting will be reflected in this policy as appropriate.

We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to respond to security incidents, including to Discord or affected users. See Discord’s Privacy Policy, GitHub’s Privacy Statement and Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Access, correction and deletion

To request access, correction or deletion of data held by the bot operator, or to raise a privacy complaint, email timestamptracker@gmail.com. Include the relevant message or interaction identifiers if available, but no credentials. We may need to verify that the request relates to your data. We will respond and delete or correct relevant data promptly where required, subject to lawful retention obligations. The operator may clear transient data by ending active sessions or restarting the bot. Original Discord messages and test posts can be managed through Discord by authorised users; contact Discord directly for data it controls.

6. Security and controls

The bot is designed to check channel access for both the requester and the bot and restrict pagination to the original requester. Access is scoped by server administrators. No system is completely secure, and the bot is currently undergoing permission testing. If you discover unauthorised access, contact us promptly. We will investigate and notify Discord and affected users as required.

7. Website and eligibility

This static website contains no operator-added analytics, tracking scripts, account forms or cookies. Hosting providers may independently collect technical information. Use of the bot is subject to Discord’s age and eligibility rules and applicable law. Do not submit protected or sensitive personal information to the bot.

8. Changes

We will update this policy when our data practices change and publish the revised date here. For material changes we will provide notice as required by applicable law and Discord’s developer rules.
